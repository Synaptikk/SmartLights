# Add a Matter light to Apple Home (§0.1) [phase 1] [verify]
Generic route for any light with a Matter logo: add it in Apple's Home app, then control it here through the Apple Home provider.

1. Open the Home app (optional)
   Open the Apple *Home* app > *+* > *Add Accessory*.
   > [Open Home <com.apple.home://>]

2. Scan the Matter code [verify]
   Scan the Matter QR code on the bulb/box.
   If the bulb is already paired to the vendor app, tap *More options* and pick it, or generate a pairing code in the vendor app (multi-admin).
   (verify: Apple Home "Turn On Pairing Mode" wording for Matter multi-admin sharing.)

3. While it pairs
   [ ] Bluetooth is on.
   [ ] You are near the device.
   [ ] Use the 2.4 GHz network during commissioning.
   [ ] Photograph the QR code; it is needed after a factory reset.

4. Back in SmartLights
   *Add brand > Apple Home* and grant HomeKit access. All Apple Home lights appear.
   Note: a native HomeKit (non-Matter) accessory can be paired to only one controller; Matter devices can be in several apps at once.

Alternatives:
- Continue with Apple Home (recommended) -> applehome
  Grant HomeKit access so every Apple Home light shows up here.

To verify:
